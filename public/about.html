Who are we?

Our mission

We are building a student prototype that helps people find nearby foodbank support and understand basic travel and item information before they set off.

Our community

Food support can be difficult to navigate on a phone, especially when people need quick answers about distance, location, and whether a foodbank lists certain items.

What we do

FoodBankFinder combines postcode search, walking guidance, item lists, and alternative recommendations in one mobile-friendly page.

The app is a prototype, so foodbank information should be checked with the foodbank or a referrer before travelling.
