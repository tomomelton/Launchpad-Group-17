About Us

FoodBankFinder is a student prototype designed to reduce confusion when people need to locate food support. It combines location, item information, and walking guidance in one simple place.

Foodbank details can change quickly, so the app is best used as a starting point alongside advice from foodbanks, referrers, or local support services.
